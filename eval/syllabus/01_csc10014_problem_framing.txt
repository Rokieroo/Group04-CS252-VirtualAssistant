COURSE SYLLABUS
Course: Problem Framing and Solution Architecture
Course Code: CSC10014
Credits: 4 (Lecture: 3, Lab: 1)
Department of Computer Science - Faculty of Information Technology

1. COURSE DESCRIPTION
This course equips students with practical thinking for enterprise problem framing, identifying the appropriate tier on the Solution Ladder, decomposing requirements into use cases, designing system architectures, and engineering human-in-the-loop automated pipelines.

2. COURSE LEARNING OUTCOMES (CLO)
- CLO1: Frame business problems, distinguish Functional vs Non-Functional Requirements, and evaluate 4 product risks (Value, Usability, Feasibility, Viability).
- CLO2: Select optimal solution tiers on the Solution Ladder ranging from manual human processes to LLM/Agent tools.
- CLO3: Decompose tasks into Use Cases, model include vs extend relationships in UML diagrams, and draft rigorous specifications.
- CLO4: Architect end-to-end success pipelines with 5 key components: Trigger, Steps, Checkpoint, Result, and Saving metrics.

3. WEEKLY SCHEDULE
Week 1: Orientation, Problem Framing, Solution Ladder, Functional vs Non-Functional Requirements.
Week 2: Task Decomposition, Anatomy of a Task, Use Case Diagram (include vs extend), MoSCoW Prioritisation.
Week 3: Pitfalls & Success Pipelines, Human-in-the-loop, Checkpoints, 4 Pipeline Patterns.
Week 4: System Architecture, API Design, Data Modeling, and Defensive Safeguards.
Week 5: Engineering Quality, Red-teaming, Benchmark Dataset Construction, and AI Evaluation.
Week 6: Midterm Milestone Report and Phase 1 Project Presentation.
Week 7: Retrieval Integration, LLM Interface, and Cite-or-Refuse Citation Strategies.
Week 8: Performance Optimization, Caching, Asynchronous Job Queues, and Background Workers.
Week 9: Staging Deployment, CI/CD, Containerization with Docker Compose, and Runtime Monitoring.
Week 10: Final Capstone Project Defense and Real-world Evaluation.

4. ASSESSMENT SCHEME
- Weekly Homework & Quizzes: 20% (due week 8).
- Midterm Project Milestone: 30% (due week 6).
- Final Capstone Project Defense: 50% (due week 10).
